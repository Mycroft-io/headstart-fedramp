How far is your ISO 27001 or SOC 2 programme from FedRAMP 20x?

Headstart 20x reads your Statement of Applicability or SOC 2 report and lays it against the 46 Key Security Indicators and the FedRAMP rules for Class B, C or D. It shows where your programme gives you a head start, where it falls short, and what 20x adds. Your files stay in your browser.
